# Monetisation Options

ClientOps Tracker can remain open source while supporting several realistic commercial paths.

## Open-Source Community Edition

Keep the core repository available under the MIT License. Community users can self-host the basic client, project, ticket, release, and dashboard workflows.

## Paid Hosted SaaS

Offer a managed service with organisation isolation, managed upgrades, backups, monitoring, email delivery, and support. Pricing could be seat-based for internal users with limits or tiers based on clients, projects, storage, and integrations.

## Deployment and Customisation

Provide a paid service for teams that want a private deployment, data migration, branding, workflow configuration, integrations, or ongoing maintenance. This is a practical early revenue path before building a larger multi-tenant service.

## Premium Features

Potential paid capabilities include advanced reporting, SLA automation, audit exports, SSO, custom fields, webhooks, email integrations, AI provider integrations, and priority support. The open-source boundary should remain clear and documented.

## Agency and Freelancer Portal Template

Agencies and freelancers could use a branded edition to manage multiple client accounts, support requests, project delivery, release notes, and handover history. Custom branding, tenant configuration, and deployment support are natural add-ons.

Any commercial offering must add production-grade tenant isolation, billing, privacy controls, data retention, backups, and support processes before handling customer data.
