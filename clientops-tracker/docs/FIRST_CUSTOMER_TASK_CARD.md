# Ten-Minute First-Visit Task

Fictional data only. Moderator provisions separate role accounts through captured
invitations and two organisations. Do not give participants the answer key or this
repository's button-by-button guide. A maintainer rehearsing these tasks is QA,
not an independent first-time study. Record consent privately; publish no identities.

| Time     | Role and goal                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0-3 min  | Administrator: prepare Harbor Agency so Nina can request shipment work and review its delivery. Canal Agency already has a project; that must not complete Harbor's setup. Tell the moderator who acts next. |
| 3-5 min  | Developer: find your shipment request, describe the agreed result, and send the delivered change for the correct client's review. Explain what your comment audience means.                                  |
| 5-8 min  | Client: open your review message while signed out. Active shipment NS-1042 still fails although a release shipped. Find what was agreed and record the appropriate decision with useful feedback.            |
| 8-10 min | Client: leave and return. Prove your decision was saved, find the delivery date and export the client-safe record. Explain the difference between ticket status, release delivery and your decision.         |

Use separate prepared cases for acceptance (all criteria met) and requested changes.
Do not lead the participant by naming controls. They may use help available in the app.
Record time, independent completion, hesitation, wrong turns and exact unclear text.
Proposed target: four of five first-time participants complete unaided; not yet an
approved acceptance target and no participant result is claimed.

3. Find Start here and hear its heading hierarchy. Select a client and verify the
   changed checklist is understandable, including recorded/incomplete status.
4. Follow the project/invitation links, submit invalid fields and locate messages.
5. Follow a signed-out review email. Verify the destination section is reachable,
   current revision/actor/date are clear and the decision controls are named.
6. Select Request changes, enter feedback, submit and locate success/history.
7. Switch comment audience and identify the matching submission action.
8. Read a summary snapshot and export; verify that internal information is absent.
9. At native browser 200% zoom and at 390px width, traverse controls without focus
   hidden beneath the header. Repeat critical decisions in both themes.
