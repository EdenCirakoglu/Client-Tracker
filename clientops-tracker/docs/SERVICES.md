## Buy Installation

The initial paid offer is **assisted installation**, not a licence to access the public code.

Proposed deliverables, subject to a written scope:

- One private deployment in the customer's agreed hosting environment.

## Software Licence

### Cancellation and privacy: private terms review

Proposed for review only, not binding terms: no payment before both parties agree scope and a slot. Before work starts, offer a reschedule or return the deposit. If the installer cannot fulfil the agreed scope, offer a new slot or refund unperformed work. For cancellation after work starts, document completed work, its agreed value, outstanding issues and any refund in writing; do not automatically retain the deposit. Do not collect the balance until the agreed acceptance record exists. Explain how to raise a problem and which verified address to use before taking payment.

Before publishing terms, record the seller's actual legal identity, country, applicable tax treatment, customer type and any mandatory cancellation/withdrawal rights. Confirm the accounting and service-record retention period and an actual privacy contact. Limit enquiry and order records to contact, agreed scope, slot, provider payment reference, amount, acceptance and handover. Stripe handles card details; never collect card numbers, hosting credentials or client ticket data in the enquiry or payment form. These proposals neither establish jurisdiction nor override statutory rights.

The [MIT licence](../LICENSE) remains unchanged. A purchase is not required to use, modify, redistribute or self-host the public code under its terms. Service payment is for the agreed work, not exclusive repository access. Preserve the required notices and applicable third-party licences.
