# Private Quote Worksheet Template

Owner-review material, not a public offer or approved price. Fill a private copy,
never commit prospect identity, provider credentials, payment details or private
quotes. Scope: one agency, one agreed environment and initial project.

| Deliverable                                 | Planning hours | Measured rehearsal hours   | Acceptance                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discovery, scope and preflight              | 2-3            | Pending                    | Supported host, access and responsibilities agreed                   |
| Installation and environment setup          | 3-5            | Pending                    | Pinned images, TLS, independent secrets and restricted roles         |
| SMTP, backups, monitoring and restore check | 4-6            | Pending                    | Approved receipt, restored backup and actual alert acceptance        |
| Accounts, project and onboarding            | 2-3            | Pending                    | Named contacts complete request-to-feedback workflow                 |
| Documentation and handover                  | 2              | Pending                    | Recovery owner, runbook and ownership signed off                     |
| Limited support allowance                   | 2              | Pending                    | Proposed allowance; hours, window, timezone and expiry need approval |
| Total                                       | **15-21**      | Pending complete rehearsal | No unbounded support commitment                                      |

Record each rehearsal's start/end, active effort, unattended elapsed time, scope,
environment and blockers. Replace each planning range only with comparable
measured work. A local cached build is **not** a measured customer installation;
do not replace 15-21 hours with CI or fixture runtime. Keep local preparation
timing as a separate diagnostic until real-service rehearsal is authorised.

Private inputs: internal hourly cost C, measured/approved hours H, one-off
provider costs P, contingency R, target gross margin M and taxes/fees T.
Working hypothesis before tax: `(H*C + P)*(1+R)/(1-M)`, with `0 <= M < 1`.
Support allowance is already in H; do not charge it twice. Any example price is
a hypothesis until Eden approves scope, price, terms and capacity.

Customer pays hosting/domain/SMTP/storage separately unless explicitly itemised.
Custom work, data migration, branding beyond the agreed mark, integrations,
ongoing maintenance, emergency response and out-of-hours support are excluded.
Software remains free MIT, with notices retained and no charge for repository access.

Owner must approve payment milestones, cancellation/refund terms, limited support
hours/timezone, response (not guaranteed resolution), handover and ongoing
operator ownership. No customer, checkout, purchase or paid installation exists
as a result of preparing this worksheet.
